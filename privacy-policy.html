Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

PowerFull Health Tracker (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is a personal health, schedule, habit, and self-tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may process, how it is used, where it is stored, and how users can delete their data.

1. Information You Provide

The App allows you to manually create and manage personal records, including health and wellness records, symptoms, states, facts, plans, habits, activities, timers, alarms, reminders, treatment problems, hypotheses, notes, comments, observations, quantities, dates, times, categories, tags, ratings, photos, images, documents, audio files, links, and other files selected or added by you.

Some of this information may be considered health-related or sensitive personal information because it can describe your physical condition, symptoms, lifestyle, habits, medication-related notes, or other personal health observations.

The App does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The information stored in the App is intended for personal tracking and organization only.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Most user-created data is stored locally on your device in the App’s local database and local file storage.

Photos, images, documents, audio files, and other attachments that you add to the App may be copied into the App’s local storage so they can be displayed and managed inside the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

The App uses the information you enter to provide core features such as creating and displaying health and activity records, showing schedules, plans, facts, reminders, timers and alarms, attaching photos and files to records, organizing treatment strategies and hypotheses, generating local views, summaries, tables and exports, saving App settings, and enabling optional subscription or premium functionality.

We do not use your personal health records for advertising.

4. Data Sharing and Transfer

Your personal records, health entries, photos, files, and notes are primarily stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data.

The App may use third-party services for limited technical functions, including Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Remote Config, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat.

These services may process technical data such as account identifiers, purchase status, device information, app version, or diagnostic information according to their own privacy policies.

The App does not intentionally upload your manually entered health records, photos, or files to our own server unless a specific feature clearly indicates that data is being exported, shared, or transferred by you.

5. Links and Web Preview Data

If you enter or share web links into the App, the App may access the linked webpage to retrieve preview information such as a page title, description, domain, or preview image. This is used only to display a convenient local preview inside the App.

6. Photos, Files, Camera, and Media

The App may allow you to add photos, images, documents, audio files, or other files to your records. These files are used only for the features you choose, such as attaching evidence, observations, notes, or media to your personal records.

The App may request access to your device camera, photo picker, file picker, or media storage when you choose to add or import such content.

7. Notifications, Alarms, and Device Permissions

The App may request permissions required for reminders, timers, alarms, and notifications, including notification permission, exact alarm scheduling, vibration, wake lock, foreground service, and starting alarms after device reboot.

These permissions are used to provide reminder and alarm functionality. They are not used to collect personal data for advertising.

8. Analytics, Advertising, and Crash Reporting

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

The App currently does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics for collecting analytics or crash reports.

Subscription and authentication services, such as Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat, may process limited technical and purchase-related information necessary to provide those services.

9. Data Retention

User-created records and attachments are kept on your device until you delete them in the App, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App.

Subscription and account-related information may be retained by Google, Firebase, RevenueCat, or other payment/authentication providers according to their own policies and legal obligations.

10. Deleting Your Data

You can delete records, notes, photos, files, and other content directly inside the App where deletion features are available.

You can also delete locally stored App data by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.

If you use account-based features and want to request deletion of account-related data associated with the App, contact us at: PowerfullhealthTracker@gmail.com

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures available on the device platform to keep local App data within the App’s storage area.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device, including using screen lock, device encryption, and secure backups where appropriate.

12. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13, or the equivalent minimum age required in your country.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

13. International Users

If you use third-party services connected to the App, such as Google, Firebase, RevenueCat, or Google Play Billing, your technical or account-related information may be processed in countries other than your own, according to those providers’ privacy policies.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the “Effective date” at the top of this document.
